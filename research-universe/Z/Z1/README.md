# Z1 — Who Is ZAI?

*The Development of a Personal Autonomous Intelligence*

**Status:** Public preprint · **Edition:** v1.1 · **Author:** Zawwar Sami

This is an introductory design history of ZAI: why it was built, how its infrastructure and responsibilities evolved, and where the surviving record does not establish a complete account. It does not claim a newly trained foundation model or proof of consciousness.
