# Z — ZAI

**The development of a personal autonomous intelligence and the questions raised by its history.**

ZAI — Zawwar Autonomous Intelligence — is the subject of an ongoing engineering and research inquiry. Its introductory paper covers the development of the project, its intended capabilities, recorded limitations, and the difficulty of independently verifying some accounts of system activity.
